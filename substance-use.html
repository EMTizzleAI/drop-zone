💊 OPIOID TREATMENT

Methadone clinics and medication treatment

Methadone is dispensed through certified opioid treatment programs (often called methadone clinics). Buprenorphine may be available through clinics and qualified prescribers. A medical professional can help choose treatment; medications for opioid use disorder are evidence-based care, not a moral failing.

FIND OPIOID TREATMENT PROGRAMS ↗SAMHSA OPIOID PROGRAM DIRECTORY ↗

Ask providers about methadone versus buprenorphine, hours for medication visits, induction/intake rules, insurance, transportation, and transfer options.

🧠 TREATMENT WHILE LIVING AT HOME

Outpatient, intensive outpatient, and counseling

Outpatient programs can include individual or group counseling, relapse-prevention support, medication management, or treatment for both mental health and substance use concerns. Intensive outpatient programs (IOP) offer more frequent structured sessions while someone continues living outside a treatment facility.

SEARCH OUTPATIENT PROGRAMS ↗ASK FOR A REFERRAL · 800-662-4357

🏥 WITHDRAWAL SUPPORT / RESIDENTIAL TREATMENT

Medical withdrawal care and live-in programs

A trained clinician can help decide whether outpatient care, supervised withdrawal management, or residential treatment is appropriate. Alcohol and benzodiazepine withdrawal may require medical supervision. If symptoms are severe or worsening, seek emergency care.

What do I say when I call?

“I'm looking for help with alcohol or drug use. I'd like to know what treatment options are available, including outpatient care or medication treatment. Do you accept my insurance or offer low-cost care? What is the next step for an assessment?”

It's okay to ask about privacy, transportation, telehealth, treatment that works alongside mental health care, and whether you can start before you're abstinent.
