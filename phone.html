WHAT ARE THOSE PHONE TABLES?

You might see people signing up customers for phones outside social-services offices or at community events. They may be representatives of companies participating in Lifeline, a government assistance program that helps eligible people afford phone or internet service.

Important: Lifeline itself is a monthly discount—not a federal promise that everyone gets a free smartphone. Some companies and states offer eligible customers $0 monthly service, and some companies include a device. Offers depend on your location and provider.

📞 TALK + TEXT

Some $0 mobile plans include unlimited calls and texts. Check the actual plan.

🌐 MOBILE DATA

Many plans include data. The amount of high-speed data and hotspot access varies.

🧾 WHO QUALIFIES?

SNAP/CalFresh, Medicaid/Medi-Cal, or qualifying low income may make you eligible.

📱 WHAT ABOUT A FREE PHONE?

Some providers supply a basic device. Others offer bring-your-own-phone service. Ask first.

MISSION 01 // GET A LINE

HERE'S WHAT TO DO

Check whether you qualify. SNAP, Medicaid, other qualifying programs, or income may count. Start at the official Lifeline site. You can ask a participating provider for application help.

Find a participating provider. Search by ZIP code or city. In California, compare state LifeLine plans, too.

Ask for the full $0 option. Get the monthly price, talk/text allowance, high-speed data amount, device cost, and any activation or optional fees before agreeing.

Test it before you leave. Place a call, send a text, and check mobile data. Save the provider name, customer-service number, and any receipt.

⚠️ MONEY-PROTECTION PROTOCOL

DON'T GET TALKED INTO SPENDING MONEY YOU NEED.

If someone offers an "upgraded" phone for an extra charge, you do not have to buy it. An upgrade might be legitimate, but it may not be worth the money—especially when your priority is simply getting a working line.

"I want the free or lowest-cost Lifeline plan. I do not want a paid upgrade. What will I owe today or each month?"

Ask the agent which phone company they represent, verify that company through an official provider directory, and read any terms before signing. Do not share your EBT PIN, banking password, or card PIN. Use an official enrollment process for sensitive identity documents.

🚩 If you're told you MUST buy a device upgrade to get a supposedly free plan, verify the offer with the provider directly before paying.

TINY DETAILS THAT MATTER

No permanent address? Ask the provider or Lifeline Support Center how to apply while unhoused. Do not invent an address.

Already receiving Lifeline? It is generally limited to one benefit per household. Check transfer rules before switching providers.

Keep using your phone. Free-to-you Lifeline service generally must be used at least once every 30 days. Recertification may also be required.

Watch out for old ACP ads. The Affordable Connectivity Program (ACP) ended in 2024. Lifeline is a different, still-active program.

Need a human? Lifeline Support Center: 800-234-9473. If you suspect fraud or improper charges, check the official complaint and support options.
